Hello! I am Lina. I am eight. I have got a cat. It is white. My brother Sami has got a dog. It is black.

Exercise 1 (4 pts)

Read and write True or False:

  1. Lina is eight.
  2. The cat is black.
  3. Sami has got a dog.
  4. The dog is white.

Exercise 2 (3 pts)

Write the missing letter: c _ t ; d _ g ; f _ sh

Exercise 3 (3 pts)

Count and write the number in letters: 2 dogs ; 5 cats ; 7 birds
