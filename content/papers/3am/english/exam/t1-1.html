Last spring, our class visited the Casbah of Algiers. It is an old city on a hill overlooking the sea. UNESCO listed it as a World Heritage Site in 1992. Our guide told us that people used to live in big houses with courtyards and fountains. We walked through narrow streets and visited the Ketchaoua Mosque. At the end of the day, we bought souvenirs and took many photos. It was an unforgettable trip.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. True or false? a) The class visited the Casbah in winter. b) The Casbah is near the sea. c) The streets of the Casbah are large.

2. Answer: a) When did UNESCO list the Casbah as a World Heritage Site? b) What did the pupils do at the end of the day?

B. Language (8 points)

1. Give the past of: visit → … ; buy → … ; take → … ; tell → …

2. Complete with "used to": In the past, people … (get) water from fountains.

3. Ask a question about the underlined words: We visited the Ketchaoua Mosque.

C. Writing (4 points)

Write a short paragraph (5–6 sentences) about a trip you made to a historical place in Algeria.
