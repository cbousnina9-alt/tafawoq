Last spring, our class went on a trip to Timgad, near Batna. The Roman emperor Trajan founded this city in the year 100. When we arrived, the guide was waiting for us at the gate. He showed us the long straight streets, the market and the big theatre. While we were walking along the main street, he told us that the city had a library and many public baths.

At noon, we had a picnic under the trees. Some pupils were taking photos while others were drawing the famous arch. Before we left, our teacher asked us to respect the site and not to write on the old stones. It was a wonderful day, and I learned a lot about the history of my country.

A. Comprehension (7 pts)

2. True or false? a) Timgad is near Batna. b) The guide arrived late. c) The city had a library. d) The pupils wrote on the stones.

3. Answer the questions: a) Who founded Timgad? b) What did the teacher ask the pupils to do?

4. What do the underlined words refer to? "He showed us…" (§1) — "it was a wonderful day" → "It" refers to…

B. Mastery of language (7 pts)

1. Find in the text words that are opposite in meaning to: modern ≠ … ; short ≠ … ; arrived ≠ …

2. Put the verbs between brackets in the correct tense: While the guide (to speak), a pupil (to ask) a question. When we (to reach) the arch, the sun (to shine).

3. Classify the words according to the pronunciation of the final "-ed": founded – showed – asked – learned – waited – walked.
