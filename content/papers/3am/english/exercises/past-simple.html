Emir Abdelkader

Emir Abdelkader was born in 1808 near Mascara, in the west of Algeria. He studied the Quran, literature and horse riding. In 1832, he became the leader of the resistance against the French colonial army. He fought for fifteen years and founded a modern state with its own army and money. He surrendered in 1847 and was taken to France. Later, he lived in Damascus, where he saved thousands of people in 1860. He died there in 1883.
