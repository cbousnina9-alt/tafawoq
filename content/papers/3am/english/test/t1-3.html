My grandmother Zohra lives in Tlemcen. When she was a child, life was very different. She didn't use to watch TV because her family didn't have one. She used to fetch water from the well with her sister every morning. In the evening, the family used to sit around the fire and listen to old stories. She walked three kilometres to school every day. Today, she says that life is easier, but people don't talk to each other as much as before.

A. Reading (08 points)

1. True or false? a) Zohra lives in Oran. b) Her family had a TV. c) She used to fetch water every morning. d) She walked to school.

2. Answer: a) What did the family use to do in the evening? b) What does Zohra think about life today?

B. Language (08 points)

1. Complete with "used to" or "didn't use to": People … (write) letters. Children … (have) mobile phones.

2. Ask questions about the underlined words: a) She walked three kilometres to school. b) She lives in Tlemcen.

3. Give the past simple of: sit – listen – say – have.

C. Writing (04 points)

Write 5 sentences comparing life in the past and life today. Use "used to" and "didn't use to".
