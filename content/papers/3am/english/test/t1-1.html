Ibn Battuta was a famous traveller. He was born in Tangier in 1304. When he was twenty-one, he left his home town to visit Mecca. Then, he travelled to many countries in Africa and Asia, such as Egypt, India and China. He visited the Maghreb too. His journey lasted about thirty years. When he came back, he told the story of his travels in a famous book.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. True or false? a) Ibn Battuta was born in Tangier. b) He left home when he was thirty. c) He visited China. d) He told the story of his travels in a book.

2. Answer: a) How long did his journey last? b) What did he do when he came back?

B. Language (8 points)

1. Give the past of: leave → … ; visit → … ; write → … ; come → …

2. Ask a question about the underlined words: He left his home town to visit Mecca.

3. Classify according to the final "-ed": travelled – visited – lasted – walked
