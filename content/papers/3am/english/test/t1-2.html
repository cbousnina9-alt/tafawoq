Lalla Fatma N'Soumer was born in 1830 in a village near Ain El Hammam, in Kabylia. She learnt the Quran when she was very young and became famous for her wisdom. When the French army attacked Kabylia, she organised the resistance with Cherif Boubaghla. She led many battles and won the respect of her people. In 1857, the French soldiers arrested her. She died in prison in 1863. Today, Algerians remember her as a great heroine.
