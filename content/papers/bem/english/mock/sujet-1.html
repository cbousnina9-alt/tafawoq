Say no to plastic bags

Every year, millions of plastic bags are thrown away in our streets, beaches and fields. They take hundreds of years to disappear, and many of them end up in the sea, where turtles and fish swallow them. Plastic also pollutes the soil and harms farmers' crops. However, we can change this situation. If we carry a cloth bag when we go shopping, we will use fewer plastic bags. Schools can organise clean-up days, and shops can stop giving free bags. Small actions, when they are done by everyone, make a big difference.

Part Two (6 points)

Written expression

Your school is starting a campaign called "A clean school". Write a short article (8–10 lines) for the school magazine to encourage pupils to reduce plastic waste. Use the conditional type 1 and "should".

A. Reading comprehension

  1. a) False: they take hundreds of years to disappear.   b) True.
  2. a) Because turtles and fish swallow them.   b) Carrying a cloth bag; clean-up days at school; shops stop giving free bags (two are enough).
  3. them → plastic bags
  4. damages = harms

B. Mastery of language

  1. Millions of plastic bags are thrown away (by people).
  2. If we recycle plastic, we will protect the environment.
  3. write: silent w — know: silent k — island: silent s.

Written expression: criteria

A title, a short introduction to the problem, practical advice with "should", at least one sentence in the conditional type 1, a conclusion, correct spelling and punctuation.
