Amina Belkacem is a fifteen-year-old pupil from Sétif. Last year, she noticed that her grandmother often forgot to take her medicine. So, she decided to invent a smart pill box. The box rings and lights up when it is time to take a pill, and it sends a message to the family's phone if the pill is not taken.

Amina worked on her project for six months with the help of her physics teacher. She used a small electronic card, a speaker and a battery. Her invention was presented at the national science fair in Algiers, and it won the first prize. Since then, she has received many invitations from universities and companies.

"I want to become an engineer and help old people live safely," she says. "Science is not only in books; it is in our daily life."
