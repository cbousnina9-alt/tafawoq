Leila is thirteen. She has a healthy lifestyle. She always has breakfast before school: some bread, some cheese and a glass of milk. She never drinks fizzy drinks. After school, she usually walks home with her friends. Twice a week, she goes swimming. She goes to bed at half past nine because she needs nine hours of sleep.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. True or false? a) Leila skips breakfast. b) She walks home after school. c) She goes swimming every day.

2. Answer: a) What does Leila have for breakfast? b) Why does she go to bed early?

3. Find in the text the opposite of: unhealthy ≠ … ; always ≠ …

B. Language (8 points)

1. Fill in with "some" or "any": Is there … milk? — I'd like … water.

2. Put the verbs in the present simple: My brother (watch) … TV in the evening. — We (not / eat) … fast food.

3. Classify according to the final "s": walks – goes – watches – sleeps

C. Writing (4 points)

Write 5 sentences about your daily routine. Use: always, usually, sometimes, never.
