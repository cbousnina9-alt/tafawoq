Sofiane is a middle school pupil. Every day, he wakes up at 6:30. He washes his face, gets dressed and has breakfast with his family. He goes to school by bus. Classes start at 8:00. After school, he plays football with his friends, then he does his homework. He usually goes to bed at 9:30.

A. Reading (7 points)

  1. Answer: a) What time does Sofiane wake up?   b) How does he go to school?
  2. Put these actions in order: does his homework — has breakfast — plays football.

B. Language (7 points)

  1. Put the verbs in the present simple: She (watch) TV every evening.   We (not / go) to school on Friday.
  2. Classify the final "-s": washes — plays — gets.
