Sami is a pupil in the second year. He usually gets up at half past six. He has breakfast with his family and goes to school by bus. Classes start at eight o'clock. In the afternoon, Sami always does his homework before he plays football. He never watches TV late because he goes to bed at nine.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. Answer the questions:

  1. What time does Sami get up?
  2. How does he go to school?
  3. What does he do before he plays football?

2. Find in the text the opposite of: always ≠ … ; morning ≠ …

B. Language (8 points)

1. Put the verbs in the present simple: My sister (go) … to school on foot. — We (not / play) … on Monday.

2. Reorder the words: often / Sami / his / visits / grandparents

3. Write the time in letters: 7:15 → … ; 9:30 → …

C. Writing (4 points)

Write 4 sentences about what you usually do at the weekend. Use adverbs of frequency.
