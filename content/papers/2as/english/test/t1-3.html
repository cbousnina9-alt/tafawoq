Read the text carefully then do the activities.

Sami is seventeen. Last year, he spent all his pocket money in the first week of every month, mostly on snacks and phone credit. Then he had nothing left for things he really wanted, like a new pair of football boots.

His older sister advised him to keep a small notebook. Each day, he wrote down what he spent. After a month, he was surprised: he had spent almost a third of his money on soft drinks! He decided to set a budget: 50% for his needs, 30% for his leisure and 20% for savings.

Six months later, Sami bought his boots without asking his parents for money. "If I hadn't written everything down, I would never have understood where my money went," he says.

A. Comprehension (8 pts)

  1. Choose the right answer: The text is about a) a football match b) managing money c) soft drinks.
  2. Answer: a) What was Sami's problem? b) Who helped him? c) How did he divide his money?
  3. Put the events in order: a) He bought his boots. b) He kept a notebook. c) He spent all his money in one week.

B. Text exploration (6 pts)

  1. Add -ful or -less to form adjectives: a) use b) care c) help
  2. Give advice with should or had better: Nadia always buys things she doesn't need.
  3. Find in the text a word that means: «money kept for the future» (§2)

C. Written expression (6 pts)

Your friend writes to you: «I never have money at the end of the month.» Write him a short email (6–8 lines) giving him three pieces of advice. Use should and had better.

A. Comprehension

1) b) managing money

2) a) He spent all his pocket money in the first week of the month. b) His older sister. c) 50% for needs, 30% for leisure and 20% for savings.

3) c → b → a

B. Text exploration

1) useful / useless ; careful / careless ; helpful / helpless (one correct form each)

2) She should make a shopping list. / She had better think before buying.

3) savings

C. Written expression

Content: three relevant pieces of advice.

Language: correct use of should / had better + base form.

Form: greeting, body, closing; coherent and clear.
