Many teenagers find it difficult to save money. They spend their pocket money on snacks, phone credit and clothes. Financial advisors say that if young people learned to make a budget, they would avoid many problems later. First, they should write down how much money they receive and spend each week. Then, they must separate needs from wants. A careful teenager who saves a little every week can buy something useful, like a bicycle or books, without asking his parents.

3. Suggest a title for the text.

B. Language (6 points)

C. Writing (4 points)

Your friend spends all his money in the first days of the month. Write him a short email (60–80 words) giving him advice. Use "should" and the conditional.
