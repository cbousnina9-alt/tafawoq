Conflicts between students happen in every school. Sometimes a small joke turns into an argument, and an argument turns into a fight. Psychologists say that most conflicts could be avoided if students listened to each other. That is why many schools now train "peer mediators": students who help their classmates discuss their problems calmly. If every school had mediators, there would be less violence and more respect.
