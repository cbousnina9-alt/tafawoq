Today is the 10th of November. It is Ilyes's birthday. He is eleven years old. His friends are at his house. His mother makes a big chocolate cake. There is orange juice, biscuits and sweets on the table. His grandfather gives him a new bike. Ilyes is very happy!

(Text written by the site)

Part One: Reading (4 pts)

  1. How old is Ilyes?
  2. What is his birthday month?
  3. True or false? a) The cake is a strawberry cake. b) His grandfather gives him a bike.

Part Two: Language (3 pts)

  1. Put the months in order: December – October – November
  2. Answer about you: How old are you?

Part Three: Writing (3 pts)

Complete the invitation card:

Dear ......,
Come to my birthday party on ...... at ...... o'clock.
See you! ......

Part One

1) He is eleven years old.

2) November.

3) a) False (chocolate cake) ; b) True.

Part Two

1) October – November – December.

2) E.g.: I am ten years old.

Part Three

A friend's name, a date, a time and the writer's name, with correct spelling.
