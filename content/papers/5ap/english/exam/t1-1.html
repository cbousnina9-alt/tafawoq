My name is Rania. I am ten years old. I have got one brother and one sister. Every day, I get up at seven o'clock. I have breakfast with my family: I drink milk and I eat bread with honey. I go to school at eight o'clock. At noon, I have lunch at home. In the evening, I do my homework and I go to bed at nine o'clock.

Part One: Reading (8 pts)

  1. True or false? a) Rania has got two sisters. b) She gets up at seven o'clock. c) She has lunch at school.
  2. What does Rania eat for breakfast?
  3. When does she go to bed?

Part Two: Language (6 pts)

  1. Complete with the correct verb: goes – eat – drinks: a) My brother ...... to school by bus. b) I ...... an apple every day. c) She ...... orange juice.
  2. What time is it? Write in letters: 8:00 → ...... ; 3:30 → ......

Part Three: Writing (6 pts)

Write 4 sentences about your daily routine (get up, have breakfast, go to school, go to bed).

Part One

1) a) False (one brother and one sister) ; b) True ; c) False (at home).

2) Bread with honey (and she drinks milk).

3) At nine o'clock.

Part Two

1) a) goes ; b) eat ; c) drinks.

2) It's eight o'clock ; It's half past three.

Part Three

Four correct sentences in the present simple (e.g. I get up at 6:30.).
