Hello! I'm Nassim. I live in a big house in Constantine. There are five rooms: a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and two bedrooms. My bedroom is small but nice. There is a bed, a desk and a big window. My cat sleeps under my bed!

Exercise 1 (6 pts)

Read and answer:

  1. Where does Nassim live?
  2. How many rooms are there in his house?
  3. Where does his cat sleep?

Exercise 2 (8 pts)

  1. Complete with "there is" or "there are": a) ...... a sofa in the living room. b) ...... two chairs in the kitchen.
  2. Choose the correct preposition: a) The book is (on / under) the desk. b) The kitchen is (next to / in) the living room.

Exercise 3 (6 pts)

Describe your bedroom in 3 sentences. Use "there is" and "there are".

Exercise 1

1) In Constantine. 2) Five rooms. 3) Under his bed.

Exercise 2

1) a) There is ; b) There are.

2) a) on ; b) next to.

Exercise 3

Three correct sentences (e.g. There is a bed. There are two pictures on the wall. There is a lamp on my desk.)
