My name is Yasmine. I am eleven years old. I live in Oran with my family. My best friend is Meriem. She has got long black hair and brown eyes. We go to school together from Sunday to Thursday. Meriem likes drawing and reading, but she doesn't like football. I like swimming!

Exercise 1 (4 pts)

Read and answer True or False:

  1. Yasmine lives in Algiers.
  2. Meriem has got brown eyes.
  3. Meriem likes football.
  4. Yasmine likes swimming.

Exercise 2 (3 pts)

Complete with am – is – has:

  1. I ...... eleven years old.
  2. Meriem ...... my friend.
  3. She ...... got long hair.

Exercise 3 (3 pts)

Put the days in order: Tuesday – Sunday – Monday. Then write what you like and what you don't like (2 sentences).

Exercise 1

1) False (Oran) ; 2) True ; 3) False ; 4) True.

Exercise 2

Exercise 3

Sunday – Monday – Tuesday.
