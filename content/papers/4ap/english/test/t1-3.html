This is my house. It is big. There are four rooms: a kitchen, a living room, a bathroom and a bedroom. There is a TV in the living room. My bed is in the bedroom.

Exercise 1 (4 pts)

Read and answer:

  1. Is the house big or small?
  2. How many rooms are there?
  3. Where is the TV?
  4. Where is the bed?

Exercise 2 (3 pts)

Complete with there is or there are: a) …… a cat in the kitchen. b) …… two chairs in my room. c) …… three windows in the classroom.

Exercise 3 (3 pts)

Put the letters in order to write the rooms: a) e d b o o m r b) t h b a m o o r c) c e h k i n t
