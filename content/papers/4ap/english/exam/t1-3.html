Hello! This is my friend Adam. He is nine years old. He has got short black hair and big brown eyes. His favourite colour is green. Adam has got a sister. Her name is Rania. She is twelve.

Exercise 1 (4 pts)

  1. How old is Adam?
  2. What colour are his eyes?
  3. What is his favourite colour?
  4. Who is Rania?

Exercise 2 (3 pts)

Write the numbers in letters: 9 – 12 – 20

Exercise 3 (3 pts)

Complete with he or she: a) Adam is my friend. …… is nine. b) Rania is his sister. …… is twelve. c) My mother is a teacher. …… is kind.
