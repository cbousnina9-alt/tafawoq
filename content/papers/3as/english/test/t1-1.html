Counterfeit products are everywhere: fake watches, false medicines, copied clothes and even imitation car parts. Counterfeiters copy famous brands to sell their goods at lower prices. Consumers who buy them often think they have made a good deal, but they are deceived. Fake medicines can be ineffective or even dangerous, and imitation car parts may cause serious accidents. Moreover, counterfeiting harms the economy: honest companies lose money and governments lose taxes. The more people buy fake goods, the more counterfeiters become rich and powerful. It is high time governments and consumers fought this illegal trade together.
