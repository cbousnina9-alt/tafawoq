Last month, our class carried out a survey about reading habits in our school. We asked 200 pupils. The results show that 60% of them read less than one book a year. Only 15% read more than five books. Most pupils say that they prefer social networks and video games. However, a few pupils told us that they read e-books on their phones. Our findings show that pupils need more time and more interesting books in the school library.

A. Reading (08 points)

1. True or false? a) The class asked 100 pupils. b) Most pupils read a lot. c) Some pupils read e-books.

2. Answer: a) How many pupils read more than five books? (give the number, not the percentage) b) What do pupils need according to the survey?

3. What do the underlined words refer to? "the results show that 60% of them…"

B. Language (08 points)

1. Write in words: 60% → … ; 1/2 → … ; 1/4 → …

2. Complete with: most – a few – none.

… of the pupils prefer video games. … pupils read e-books. … of the pupils said they hate the library.

3. Turn into reported speech: The pupils said: "We need more interesting books."

C. Writing (04 points)
