Social networks have become part of teenagers' daily life. They use them to chat with friends, share photos and follow the news. Some pupils also join study groups where they exchange lessons and exercises. However, these networks can be dangerous. Many teenagers spend more time online than with their families, and some sleep less because they stay connected late at night. Experts advise young people to limit their screen time and to protect their personal information.

A. Reading (10 points)

1. True or false? a) Teenagers use social networks only to play games. b) Some pupils study through social networks. c) Experts advise teenagers to stay connected at night.

2. Answer: a) Give two advantages of social networks mentioned in the text. b) What do experts advise young people to do?

3. Who or what does the underlined word refer to? they → …

4. Find in the text words closest in meaning to: risky = … ; recommend = …

B. Language (6 points)

1. Give the correct form of the adjectives: Social networks are (popular) … than television. — This is the (useful) … website I know.

2. Rewrite using "as … as": My phone is cheaper than yours. → Your phone is not …

C. Writing (4 points)

Write a short paragraph (60–80 words) about the advantages and disadvantages of social networks. Use sequencers.
