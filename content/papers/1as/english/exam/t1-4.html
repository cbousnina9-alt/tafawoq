Last Saturday, Amine was walking to the stadium when he realised that his phone was not in his pocket. He went back to the bus station, but he could not find it. While he was asking the guard for help, a young girl came towards him. She was holding his phone! She had found it on a seat in the bus. Amine thanked her warmly and offered her a drink, but she refused politely. “I only did what anyone should do,” she said.

Reading comprehension (7 pts)

  1. Where was Amine going?
  2. Where did the girl find the phone?
  3. What did Amine offer her?
  4. What do the underlined words refer to: “She” (line 4), “it” (line 5)?
  5. Give a title to the text.

Mastery of language (6 pts)

  1. Put the verbs in brackets in the correct tense: While I (read), the lights (go) out.
  2. Join with “when”: He was sleeping. The phone rang.
  3. Find in the text the opposite of: “found” ; “impolitely”.

Written expression (7 pts)

Write a short paragraph (8–10 lines) about a surprising event that happened to you, using the past simple, the past continuous, “when” and “while”.

Reading comprehension

1) He was going to the stadium.

2) She found it on a seat in the bus.

3) He offered her a drink.

4) “She” refers to the young girl ; “it” refers to the phone.

5) Example: “An honest girl” / “The lost phone”.

Mastery of language

1) While I was reading, the lights went out.

2) He was sleeping when the phone rang.

3) found ≠ lost ; impolitely ≠ politely.

Written expression

Relevance: a clear past event with a beginning, a surprise and an end.

Language: correct use of the past simple and past continuous, when / while, spelling.

Coherence and presentation: linking words, punctuation, neatness.
