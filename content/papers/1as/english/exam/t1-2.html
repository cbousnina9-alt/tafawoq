Dear Amina,

Thank you for your nice letter. I'm writing to you from my bedroom in Montreal. It's very cold here now, and it's snowing outside! I go to a big secondary school near my house. I usually take the bus, but today my father is driving me because the streets are icy.

At school, I study French and English because both languages are official in Canada. My favourite subject is biology. In my free time, I play ice hockey with my friends and I read a lot. This month, I'm reading a book about the Sahara. It's fascinating! Is it really very hot there in summer?

Please tell me more about your town, your school and the dishes you eat. Write soon!

Best wishes,
Sarah

Part One: Reading (15 points)

A. Comprehension (08 pts)

1. The text is: a) an article b) a letter c) a dialogue.

2. Are these statements true or false? a) Sarah lives in Montreal. b) It's hot in Canada now. c) Sarah's favourite subject is French. d) Sarah wants to know about the Sahara.

3. Answer the questions: a) How does Sarah usually go to school? b) Why is her father driving her today? c) What does Sarah do in her free time?

4. What do the underlined words refer to? "both languages" → … ; "It's fascinating" → …

B. Text exploration (07 pts)

1. Find in the text words closest in meaning to: interesting = … ; frozen = …

2. Put the verbs between brackets in the right tense: Every Friday, we (to visit) our grandparents. Look! The children (to play) in the snow.

3. Ask the questions that the underlined words answer: a) She plays ice hockey with her friends. b) She reads a book about the Sahara.

4. Classify the words according to the pronunciation of their final "s": books – friends – languages – streets.

Part Two: Written expression (05 points)

Write a reply to Sarah (10–12 lines). Tell her about your town, your school, your free time and a traditional Algerian dish. Answer her question about the Sahara.
