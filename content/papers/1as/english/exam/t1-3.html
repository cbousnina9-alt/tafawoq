Once upon a time, in a small village between two mountains, there lived a poor fisherman called Hamid. One evening, while he was fishing in the lake, he heard a soft voice. He looked around but saw nobody. Suddenly, a golden fish jumped out of the water and spoke to him: "Please let me go, and I will help your village."

Hamid was kind, so he put the fish back into the water. The next morning, as soon as the villagers woke up, they found that the dry fields near the lake were green again. From that day on, the village never suffered from drought. When people asked Hamid about the miracle, he only smiled and said, "Kindness always comes back to you."
