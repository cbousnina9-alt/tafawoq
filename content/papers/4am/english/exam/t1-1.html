A wonderful day

My name is Yacine. I am fifteen and I live in Oran. I have studied English for four years. Last summer, I visited my uncle in Tlemcen. We went to the Lalla Setti plateau and we had a picnic with my cousins. It was a wonderful day. I have never seen such a beautiful view! Next year, I will take the BEM exam, so I am working hard.
