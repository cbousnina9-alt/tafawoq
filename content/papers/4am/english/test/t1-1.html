My name is Yasmine. I'm fourteen and I live in Béjaïa. People say I am friendly and generous because I often help my classmates with their lessons. I love reading detective stories and I enjoy swimming in summer. However, I hate waiting in queues: I'm a bit impatient! My best friend Lyna is different. She is calm and shy, and she prefers drawing and listening to music.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. Answer the questions: a) Why do people say Yasmine is generous? b) What does she hate? c) What does Lyna prefer?

2. Find in the text the opposite of: unfriendly ≠ … ; patient ≠ …

B. Language (8 points)

1. Put the verbs in the correct form: I love (read) … ; Lyna (prefer) … drawing.

2. Add the prefix "un" or "im" to get the opposite: polite → … ; kind → … ; possible → … ; happy → …

3. Reorder the words: always / helps / she / her / classmates

C. Writing (4 points)

Describe your personality in about 6 sentences: two qualities, one weak point, what you love and hate doing.
