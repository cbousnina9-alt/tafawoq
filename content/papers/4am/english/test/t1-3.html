Hi, I'm Amira. I'm fourteen and I live in Tlemcen. I'm quite shy, but I'm very hard-working and generous. I'm good at drawing and I can play the piano, but I can't swim. My brother Walid is the opposite of me: he is outgoing and funny. He is good at playing football, but he isn't good at drawing at all!

Part One (13 pts)

  1. Answer the questions: a) Where does Amira live? b) What is she good at? c) What can't she do?
  2. Find in the text the opposite of: a) shy ≠ ...... ; b) lazy ≠ ......
  3. Complete with "can" or "can't": a) A fish ...... swim. b) A baby ...... drive a car.
  4. Put the verbs in the correct form: a) Walid is good at (play) ...... football. b) I enjoy (read) ...... stories.

Part Two (7 pts)

Write a short paragraph (50–60 words) about yourself: your personality, what you can do and what you are good at.
