Dear Karim,

My name is Lucas and I'm fourteen. I live in Lyon, in France. I'm a sociable and generous boy, but sometimes I'm a little lazy! I usually get up at seven and I go to school by bike. I love reading comics and I never miss a basketball match on TV. At the moment, I'm writing to you from my bedroom and my sister is playing the guitar next door. Please tell me about yourself and your country.

Best wishes,
Lucas

Part One (14 pts)

  1. Answer the questions: a) How old is Lucas? b) How does he go to school? c) What is his sister doing now?
  2. Find in the text two adjectives that describe Lucas's personality.
  3. Find the opposite of: a) hard-working ≠ …… ; b) always ≠ ……
  4. Put the verbs in the right tense (present simple or present continuous): a) Look! The children (play) …… in the garden. b) My father (work) …… every day.
  5. Put the adverb in the right place: "I am late for school. (never)"

Part Two (6 pts)

Write a short reply to Lucas (6–8 lines): introduce yourself, describe your personality and your daily routine.
