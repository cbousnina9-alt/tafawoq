Hello! My name is Ines. I am eleven years old. I am Algerian. I live in Sétif. This is my new friend. His name is Karim. He is twelve. He is from Oran. His favourite colour is blue. We are in the same class.

A. Reading (8 points)

1. True or false?

  1. Ines is twelve years old.
  2. Ines lives in Sétif.
  3. Karim is from Oran.
  4. Karim and Ines are in different classes.

2. Answer the questions: a) How old is Karim? b) What is his favourite colour?

B. Language (8 points)

1. Fill in with am / is / are: I … a pupil. — She … from Annaba. — They … friends.

2. Fill in with my / his / her: This is Ali. … name is Ali. — This is Lina. … mother is a teacher.

3. Write the numbers in letters: 13 → … ; 20 → …
