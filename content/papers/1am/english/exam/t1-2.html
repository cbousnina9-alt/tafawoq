Hi! I'm Adam. I'm a pupil at Ibn Khaldoun Middle School in Médéa. My school is big. There are twenty classrooms, a library and a sports ground. The library is next to the headmaster's office. I study English on Sunday and Wednesday. My favourite teacher is Mrs Benali. She is kind and funny.

1. True or false? a) Adam's school is small. b) There is a library in the school. c) Adam studies English on Monday.

3. Write the plural: a library → … ; a class → …
