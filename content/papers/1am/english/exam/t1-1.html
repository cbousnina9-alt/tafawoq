Hello! My name is Amina. I am eleven years old. I am from Constantine. My father is a doctor and my mother is a teacher. I have got one brother. His name is Karim. He is eight. My favourite colour is green and I like reading stories.

A. Reading (7 points)

  1. True or false? a) Amina is twelve.   b) Her mother is a teacher.
  2. Answer: a) Where is Amina from?   b) What is her brother's name?

B. Language (7 points)

  1. Complete with am, is or are: I ...... a pupil.   They ...... friends.   She ...... from Oran.
  2. Complete with my, his or her: Karim likes ...... bike.   Amina loves ...... mother.
  3. Write the plural: a book → two ......   a box → three ......

C. Written expression (6 points)

Introduce yourself in 4 sentences: your name, age, town and favourite colour.
